Two common situations call for a quick step from you before our technician gets there. The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or gives off a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and call us. That small step can save you from a much larger repair.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Scranton

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

Take a look at the age of your system. Its age says a lot about what comes next. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and also shows rising bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement in peak season.

Minor issues rarely stay small when they're ignored. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, inexpensive repair, but left alone for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a few miserable nights.
